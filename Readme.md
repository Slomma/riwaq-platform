📖 RIWAQ | Digital Library & Research Platform

RIWAQ is a digital platform designed to connect students, researchers, authors, readers, and creative service providers in one integrated environment.

The platform combines a digital library, scientific research services, author profiles, social interaction, and professional book and document design services.

🌐 Main Features

- 📚 Digital library for books, notes, research papers, and educational materials.
- 🔬 Scientific research support center.
- 👨‍🔬 Researcher network covering different scientific fields and specialties.
- ✍️ Author profiles and publishing tools.
- 👤 One account with multiple roles.
- 👥 Follow authors and researchers.
- 💬 Private messaging and conversations.
- 🔔 Real-time notifications.
- 🎨 Book and document design service starting from $5.
- 🛒 Marketplace for books and research materials.
- ❤️ Favorites and saved content.
- 💬 Comments and interactions.
- ⭐ Ratings and reviews.
- 📦 Order tracking and service management.

👤 Multi-Role User Accounts

A single user account can have multiple roles, depending on permissions and approval:

- 🎓 Student
- 🔬 Researcher
- ✍️ Author
- 🎨 Service Provider

Users do not need separate accounts for each role.

For example, a researcher can also become an author using the same account and profile.

🔬 Scientific Research Center

Students can submit research-related requests by providing:

- Research topic
- Scientific field
- Specialty
- Academic level
- Required service
- Language
- Expected deadline
- Additional requirements

The system can match requests with available researchers according to their specialties and areas of expertise.

Research services may include:

- Research topic development
- Literature and source research
- Academic organization
- Data analysis
- Referencing and citation support
- Language and academic proofreading
- Research formatting
- Research review

The platform is intended to support legitimate academic and research work. Users remain responsible for complying with their institution's academic integrity and authorship requirements.

✍️ Authors

Authors can create professional profiles and publish their books, notes, research materials, and other eligible content.

Published content can appear in:

- The main library
- Categories
- Search results
- The author's profile
- Followers' feeds

Authors can also receive followers, comments, ratings, and notifications.

🎨 Book & Document Design Service

RIWAQ provides a professional design service for books, notes, research papers, educational materials, and other documents.

Starting Price

$5

Customers can submit a design request and communicate directly with the service provider to discuss requirements and negotiate the final price when necessary.

     ↓

Each order receives a unique order number and can generate notifications for both the customer and service provider.

🛒 Digital Marketplace

Eligible books, research materials, educational resources, and other digital content may be offered for free or for a fee.

Each transaction can record:

- Item price
- Buyer
- Author
- Researcher
- Content owner
- Platform share
- Payment status
- Settlement status

Revenue-sharing rules can be configured by the platform administrator according to the applicable agreement for each project.

💬 Communication

RIWAQ includes an internal communication system that can support:

- User-to-user messaging
- Student-researcher communication
- Customer-service communication
- Author-reader communication
- Order-related conversations

🔔 Notifications

Users can receive notifications for:

- New messages
- New research requests
- Design requests
- Order updates
- New comments
- New followers
- New books from followed authors
- Payment updates
- Delivery updates

👤 User Profiles

Every user has a personal profile containing information appropriate to their selected roles.

A profile may include:

- Name
- Profile picture
- Biography
- Country
- Areas of expertise
- Published books
- Research projects
- Services
- Followers
- Following
- Ratings
- Reviews

🛡️ Content & Copyright

RIWAQ is intended to host original, licensed, or legally distributable content.

Users must have the appropriate rights or permissions to upload and publish materials.

The platform should include mechanisms for reporting copyright violations and inappropriate content.

🛠️ Technology

The initial version is being developed with:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

Future versions may include:

- Authentication
- Database
- Secure file storage
- Real-time messaging
- Notifications
- Payment processing
- Role-based access control
- Digital content protection
- Administrative dashboard

🚧 Project Status

Currently in development.

The project will be developed step by step, starting with the user interface and core pages, followed by authentication, database integration, research requests, author management, messaging, notifications, payments, and secure digital delivery.

🎯 Vision

RIWAQ aims to create an integrated digital environment where students, researchers, authors, readers, and creative professionals can discover, create, publish, collaborate, and access educational and research resources.
